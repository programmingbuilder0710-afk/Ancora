Using Doer

  1. 1. Type your goal.

    Anything from “run a half marathon by November 30” to “learn conversational Spanish.” A deadline helps; notes help more.

  2. 2. Connect your calendar.

    Google or Outlook in two taps, or paste a calendar link (iCloud, Fastmail, Proton…). Doer looks at when you're busy so it never double-books you. Optional — it also works with just your working hours.

  3. 3. Tap “Plan tasks.”

    Doer drafts the sessions — “Easy run + mobility, 45 min” — and schedules each one into real free time on a “My Plan” calendar. Your existing events are never touched.

When the week goes sideways

Didn't start a session?

Tap “Didn't start” and it hops to the next free slot — instantly, even offline.

Life happened?

Tap “Re-plan.” Everything unfinished gets re-dated into your current free time, plus a short note on pacing.

Marked one done by accident?

Tap the green check to un-complete. The task comes back and re-fits itself into your week.

Too busy this week?

Tasks that don't fit pile up under “needs attention” instead of vanishing. Clear a gap and re-plan.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Planning runs on a shared free AI quota. If it runs out, paste your own key from OpenRouter, OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible provider in Settings.

Where does my data live?

On your device — there's no account and no tracking. Goals and tasks stay in your browser (or phone). To draft tasks, your goal text and a summary of your free time are sent to an AI model. Calendar sign-in is handled by our connection provider, Pipedream; Doer's server stores nothing.

Does the AI pick my times?

No. The AI only drafts the tasks and suggests days. A scheduler on your device places them in your working hours, around your meetings, with a buffer between sessions.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — the web app works in any phone browser. An Android app that writes straight to your phone's calendar is in progress.
