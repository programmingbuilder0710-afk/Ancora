How it works

  1. Name the goal.

    “Run a half marathon by November 30.” Add a priority, a deadline, and any notes or training plans you already have.

  2. Doer drafts the sessions.

    AI breaks the goal into tasks like “Easy run + mobility, 45 min” and suggests which day each one belongs on.

  3. Your calendar decides the time.

    A scheduler on your device places every session in real free time, respecting your working hours, buffers, and existing events.

Works with the calendar you already use

Connect Google Calendar or Outlook, or paste a calendar link from iCloud, Fastmail, Proton, or anywhere that shares .ics. Doer reads when you're busy and writes your plan to a separate “My Plan” calendar. It never edits your other events.

Private by default

There's no account and no tracking. Goals and tasks live in your browser or on your phone. When you plan, your goal, any notes you attached, and a summary of your free time are sent to the AI model to draft tasks. If you connect a calendar, the sign-in is held by our connection provider, Pipedream; Doer's server only relays requests to read when you're busy and to write your plan, and stores nothing. Prefer your own AI key? Add it in Settings; it's stored on your device.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Planning uses a shared free AI quota. If it runs out, add your own key from OpenRouter, OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible provider.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start. Because your data stays on this device, plans don't sync between devices yet.

Which calendars work?

Google Calendar and Outlook connect directly. iCloud, Fastmail, Proton, Zoho and anything else that can share an .ics link work by link, and you can import .ics files.

Does the AI decide when I work?

No. The AI decides what the tasks are and roughly which day. Exact times come from a deterministic scheduler that follows your hours and busy time.

Is there a phone app?

An Android version that writes straight to your phone's calendar is in progress. The web app works on any phone browser today.
